About the University of Bulgaria

The University of Bulgaria, founded in 1889, stands as a distinguished institution of higher learning, nestled in the heart of Sofia, Bulgaria. Situated in a beautiful, historic city known for its rich cultural heritage and vibrant intellectual atmosphere, the University of Bulgaria has grown into a prominent center for research, education, and cultural exchange.
From its humble beginnings as a modest private institution, the University of Bulgaria has evolved into a comprehensive system encompassing more than 15 faculties and centers, offering a wide array of undergraduate, graduate, and doctoral programs across various disciplines. With a strong commitment to excellence, the university is dedicated to fostering a culture of inquiry, innovation, and collaboration, preparing its students for successful careers and further academic pursuits.

The campus of the University of Bulgaria is a testament to its commitment to beauty and functionality, featuring modern facilities, research laboratories, and academic centers that cater to the diverse needs of its students and faculty. The university's libraries, housing millions of books and digital resources, are a vital resource for scholars and students alike.

The University of Bulgaria is also known for its vibrant student life, with numerous clubs, societies, and extracurricular activities that promote personal growth, social interaction, and cultural enrichment. The university fosters a collaborative and inclusive environment, encouraging students from diverse backgrounds to engage with one another and contribute to a rich tapestry of ideas and perspectives.

Through its research initiatives, the University of Bulgaria plays a significant role in advancing knowledge and contributing to the global community. Faculty and students engage in groundbreaking research in areas such as science, technology, engineering, mathematics, humanities, and social sciences, producing impactful publications and developing innovative solutions to pressing global challenges.

The University of Bulgaria is a proud member of the European Higher Education Area (EHEA) and is accredited by the European Association for Quality Assurance in Higher Education (ENQA). It is also a member of the Association of European Universities (AEU), which further underscores its commitment to excellence and international standards.
As the University of Bulgaria continues to thrive, it remains dedicated to its mission of educating, researching, and contributing to the advancement of knowledge. With a rich history, a dynamic community, and a strong commitment to innovation, the university stands as a beacon of intellectual excellence and a driving force for progress in Bulgaria and beyond.
